Platform

Three tools. One unified platform.

Everything clinical operations teams need to reduce friction, improve patient outcomes, and process claims with confidence.

Jargon Decoder

Clinical visit notes rewritten in plain English — ICD-10 codes explained, medications contextualized, follow-up steps clarified so patients can act.

  • Named entity extraction with confidence scoring
  • RAG-grounded explanations from medical knowledge base
  • Urgency classification: Routine / Soon / Urgent

Insurance Matcher

Rule-based scoring across Medicare, Medicaid, ACA, CHIP, ESI, and HDHP+HSA — with an AI overlay that generates personalized, policy-grounded guidance.

  • Deterministic eligibility engine across 6 plan types
  • Retrieved policy documents injected into AI context

Claims scored across six signals and routed to the right model tier. Structured decisions — approved, pending, or denied — with source-cited reasoning and appeal paths.

  • Complexity scoring across 6 weighted signals

  • Step-by-step appeal guidance on every denial
Process

How Synthure works

Every request flows through a staged pipeline — validated, enriched with retrieved context, then generated with a hard output schema enforced by Claude tool use.

01

Quality Gate

ICD-10 and CPT format validation, SHA-256 deduplication, and entity confidence scoring before any LLM call is made.

→
02

Entity Extraction

Claude Haiku extracts medical entities — diagnoses, medications, procedure codes — each with a confidence score. Never generates prose.

→
03

RAG Retrieval

BM25 retrieval over 80+ knowledge base documents — ICD-10 profiles, denial patterns, policy rules — injected as numbered context blocks.

→
04

Gated Generation

Claude generates via forced tool use. Every claim must cite a retrieved document ID. Hallucinated citations are stripped post-generation.

Architecture

Built for production clinical workflows

Typed intermediate representations, a data quality gate, and multi-agent model routing that sends complex cases to frontier models and routine work to lighter ones — reducing cost without sacrificing accuracy.
